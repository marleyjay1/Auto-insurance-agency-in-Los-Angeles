What Coverage Should You Consider?

A good auto insurance policy typically includes:

  • Liability Insurance: Covers damages you cause to others in an accident.
  • Collision Coverage: Pays for repairs to your vehicle after a crash.
  • Comprehensive Coverage: Protects against non‑collision incidents like theft or weather damage.
  • Uninsured/Underinsured Motorist Protection: Safeguards you when the other driver lacks adequate insurance.
  • Medical Payments or Personal Injury Protection: Helps with medical expenses after an accident.
